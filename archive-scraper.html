Archive Link Scraper.

Extract direct MP4 links from an Internet Archive item, uploader, favourites, collection or search. Choose your results and export parser-ready TXT files in one ZIP. No Python server or account required.

Ready. Browser requests depend on Internet Archive allowing cross-origin access.

Choose which TXT lists to include in your ZIP.

About this tool

The scraper uses Internet Archive's public metadata and search endpoints directly in your browser. If a request fails, its error appears above. The original Python version remains available in tools/archive-link-scraper/.

Bulk results are limited to 100 items per search to keep the browser responsive. ZIP files contain TXT link lists, not MP4 video files.
