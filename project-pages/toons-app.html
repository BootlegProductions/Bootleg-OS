Toons App Project

A colourful concept for a cartoon and music streaming service, developed through branding, screen mock-ups and an animated promotional storyboard.

Video & Post Production · University

The idea

Toons imagined a single home for classic cartoons, their soundtracks, theme songs, animated music videos and related music. The project explored how the service could look on a website and mobile screen, then how a short advert could introduce it. The concept eventually fed into Bootleg Broadcast.

Concept design Storyboarding Brand identity Motion graphics

Finding the visual language

The starting point was a mix of music-video energy, classic cartoon graphics and the familiar browsing patterns of streaming platforms. Mood boards brought together colour palettes, type, textures and layouts. I tested logo directions and made screens to show how a viewer might discover the service across desktop and mobile.

Pitching it in motion

For the revised Video & Post Production concept, I developed a twelve-scene promotional sequence. It opens with someone bored by a familiar television service. Colour and music return as the screen is taken over by a “stay tooned” message, radial cartoon transitions and a list of the proposed content: cartoons, soundtracks, theme songs, AMVs and music videos.

The advert then moves to a phone close-up. A tap triggers animated effects before the Toons logo and app appear together in the final shot. I planned the sequence as a two-column audio/action script and a storyboard, so camera moves, text entrances and transitions had a clear purpose before editing began.

From Toons to Bootleg Broadcast

University coursework kept this idea mostly within mock-ups, presentations and a promotional sequence. I enjoyed the concept too much to leave it there. I kept thinking about how a service for cartoons, music and accidental discovery could actually work, and that thread eventually developed into Bootleg Broadcast.

Bootleg Broadcast is a different, broader project: a working web television with channels, schedules and an interactive CRT interface. But Toons was one of the early places I tried to express the appeal of mixing cartoons, music and the feeling of switching on something unexpected.

Project pathThe university version survives as a sketchbook, logo studies, website mock-ups and demonstration videos. The idea continued after the assignment in the live Bootleg Broadcast project.
