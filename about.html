About Oliver Lewis

Websites, useful tools and a love of digital oddities.

I’m Oliver, a web designer and creative developer. I’ve been building websites for close to ten years, combining code, graphics, animation and video to make things I actually want to use.

Explore my projects Get in touch

It started with spare parts

My dad loved computers and all things Apple. For several years, our living room doubled as a busy phone repair shop, with broken iPhones, replacement screens and batteries passing through it. We built my first computer together from spare parts and whatever we could salvage. Technology always felt like something you could take apart, understand and make your own.

From the command line to creative work

At school, computer science started with binary and hexadecimal. Things became much more interesting when we moved onto Ubuntu, the command line, HTML and CSS. At college, specialising in web design opened up graphic design, animation, databases, video editing and post production alongside development.

I went on to graduate with a 2:1 BSc with Honours in Computer Science. My final-year project poster won the best poster design award after being displayed and voted on by students and staff.

Still learning by making

Education gave me a foundation, but the work I do in my own time is where I keep stretching my skills. Bootleg OS brings those experiments together: practical image tools, interactive apps, project archives, artwork and writing, with a visual style inspired by retro computing and the media I grew up with.

Built to be used

Most of my projects begin with something I want for myself. Building it, testing it and improving it keeps my skills moving forward—and gives other people something useful to try.
